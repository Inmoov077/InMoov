Marwadi University is located on the Rajkot-Morbi highway in Rajkot, Gujarat, India. It was founded in 2009 by the Marwadi Education Foundation. The university has secured NAAC A++ accreditation, which is the highest score awarded by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council of India. It has been recognized as a Center of Excellence by the Government of Gujarat. The university boasts state-of-the-art campus facilities, including modern theatre-style ICT classrooms, a massive central library housing over 50,000 reference books, separate residential hostels for over 3,500 students, full medical clinics, a sports complex, and dining options. It is home to students from over 54 countries, creating a global learning environment.

In terms of rankings and achievements, Marwadi University made a historic debut in the QS Asia University Rankings 2026 at Rank 353, which is the highest debut rank ever for an Indian university, and ranked in the top 100 in Asia for both Internationalisation and Faculty Quality. In the QS World University Rankings by Subject 2026, it is ranked in the 751-850 band for Computer Science & Information Systems. Nationally, the university consistently ranks well in the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF), placing in the 101-150 band for universities, the 201-300 band for Engineering, and the 102-125 band for Pharmacy. Many of its engineering courses hold NBA Tier-1 accreditation.

The university maintains an active International Relations Office and has established over 45 global partnerships and MoUs with major foreign institutions, such as Webster University in the USA, Cattolica University in Italy, Bloomsbury Institute in the UK, and the University of Dunaujvaros in Hungary. These alliances support semester exchange programs, summer/winter schools, dual-degree courses, joint research, and tuition fee waivers. Students also receive support to win international scholarships like Erasmus+, DAAD, NAWA, and MITACS.

The MU Robotics & AI Club was founded in 2018. It is located in the Main Building, Room MB106, and is open 24/7 for students to innovate. The club is a central hub for student projects in robotics, machine learning, and automation, boasting a community of over 500 active members. The club provides advanced prototyping labs, 3D printers, and development boards, along with expert workshops on NVIDIA Jetson Nano, Robot Operating System (ROS), MATLAB, and Simulink. Students participate in prestigious hackathons, file patents, and publish research papers.

Key projects built by the Robotics & AI Club include the Smart Water Cleaning Boat, which is an autonomous, solar-powered waste collector that reached the finals in the Smart India Hackathon. Another key project is the Flipkart Smart Inventory Tracker, which uses computer vision to track shelf inventory and recognize item damage in real-time, placing in the national top 50 in the Flipkart Grid Challenge. The club also developed an AI-driven Smart Home Automation System that won first prize at D.Y. Patil, an IoT-enabled 3 DOF Robotic Arm, and a low-cost myoelectric Bionic Hand controlled by muscle sensors for prosthetics.

The Department of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning & Data Science (AI, ML & DS) at Marwadi University focuses on advanced computing education. The department offers courses in Deep Learning, Big Data Analytics, Computer Vision, and Neural Networks, and houses the PARAM SHAVAK high-performance supercomputing facility to support intensive computing research. The department hosts regular national technical events, including the Intellify Hackathon, to promote student prototype developments.

Professor Akshay Ranpariya is an Assistant Professor and Ph.D. researcher in the Computer Engineering department who serves as the administrator of the Robotics & AI Club at Marwadi University. In his administrative role, he manages the club labs, guides students on developing AI prototypes, filing patents, publishing papers, and organizing hackathons like Intellify. His research interests focus on Artificial Intelligence, embedded systems, and international research collaborations.

Dr. Rajendrasinh (R.B.) Jadeja is the Provost and Pro Vice-Chancellor of Marwadi University, and a Professor in the Faculty of Engineering. Dr. Jadeja is a distinguished academic leader who has held prominent roles in the IEEE Gujarat Section and IEEE Region-10, contributing to global engineering research. He advocates for Outcome-Based Education (OBE), experiential learning, and integrating cutting-edge research and international collaborations into engineering curriculums.

Dr. Madhu Shukla is a Professor, Associate Dean, and Head of the Department of Computer Engineering - AI & Big Data Analytics at Marwadi University. Her research focuses on using Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Big Data Analytics in Healthcare, particularly in cardiovascular disease prediction and digital pathology. She is an editor for books on medical decision support systems and serves on the technical program committees of multiple international conferences.
